## Services

<article class="feature-card">
  <h2 class="feature-card__title">Expert Witness and Litigation Support</h2>
  <p class="feature-card__role"><strong>For law firms and legal teams</strong></p>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    I analyze patents for infringement and validity. I review source code. I write technical reports and declarations for legal teams. My subjects are cryptography, lattice mathematics, cryptographic software, and hardware accelerators.
  </p>
</article>

<article class="feature-card">
  <h2 class="feature-card__title">Technical Due Diligence</h2>
  <p class="feature-card__role"><strong>For investors and acquirers</strong></p>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    I examine the technical claims of a company or a product. I can tell you which performance numbers are real. I can also tell you how each measurement was made. I can also tell you what a system does and what it does not do.
  </p>
</article>

<article class="feature-card">
  <h2 class="feature-card__title">Technical Advisory</h2>
  <p class="feature-card__role"><strong>For engineering and research teams</strong></p>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    I advise teams on cryptographic architecture and on the design of accelerators. Typical subjects are fully homomorphic encryption (FHE), post-quantum cryptography (PQC), secure multi-party computation (MPC), and confidential computing.
  </p>
</article>

<article class="feature-card">
  <h2 class="feature-card__title">Executive Briefings and Training</h2>
  <p class="feature-card__role"><strong>For leadership teams and technical staff</strong></p>
  <p class="feature-card__body">
    I give briefings to leadership teams. I also teach courses of one day or two days for technical staff. Subjects include FHE, PQC migration, and privacy-preserving machine learning.
  </p>
</article>
